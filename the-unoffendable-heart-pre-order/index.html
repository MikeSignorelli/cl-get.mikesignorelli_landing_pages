About the book

Stop letting offense shape your life and start living free from it

We're exhausted. The relationships that should bring life feel like a minefield. The workplace is hostile, the church is divided, and our hearts have grown bitter despite our best prayers. Somewhere along the way, we started believing that's just how life is.

Providing a powerful, practical antidote to the epidemic of offense, Pastor Mike Signorelli shares his own vulnerable stories alongside solid biblical teaching and hands-on application to help you develop genuine spiritual resilience, including how to:

→Deal with difficult people without losing yourself
→Set realistic expectations of imperfect people
→Heal the wounds that leave you vulnerable to offense
→Move from a victim mentality to a victor mentality
→Break free from cycles of outrage and resentment
→Engage fully in relationships while staying spiritually protected

Jesus didn't survive a life full of betrayal, rejection, and injustice by having thicker skin. He had a different kind of heart altogether, and according to Scripture, that same heart is available to you.

This book shows you how to get there.

About the author

Mike Signorelli

Mike Signorelli is a pastor, author, and global Bible teacher known for blending traditional ministry with modern platforms that reach millions. As the founding lead pastor of V1 Church, one of the fastest-growing churches in the United States, he leads a multi-campus movement spanning New York, New Jersey, Miami, and beyond. He is also the host of The Narrow Way on Trinity Broadcasting Network and Daystar, and his digital ministry now connects with over ten million people each month.

Through initiatives like V1 College and the MVMNT Network, Mike equips leaders and believers around the world. His influence extends through bestselling books like Inherit Your Freedom and the nationally released film The Domino Revival. Together with his wife Julie, he also leads V1 Community Impact, a nonprofit providing food distribution, disaster relief, and community support to families across the United States.
